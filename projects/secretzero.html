Case study · Privileged access

SecretZero: privileged access with no standing secrets

A Zero Standing Privilege PAM platform I designed and built at SecurITaaS Pte Ltd. Administrators get short-lived certificates instead of stored passwords, so there is nothing long-lived to steal.

Passwordless RDP code
SecretZero belongs to SecurITaaS Pte Ltd, and two patent applications are pending in Singapore. This page describes the design at a high level only. The passwordless RDP component is published under the Business Source License.

The problem

Traditional PAM vaults passwords and rotates them, but the passwords still exist and stay valid between rotations. Every vaulted credential is standing privilege: if the vault, an endpoint or a session is compromised, the attacker inherits it. The same pattern is now repeating with non-human identities and AI agents, which are often handed long-lived keys.

What I built

  • Short-lived certificates instead of passwords. An in-house certificate authority issues credentials only when access is needed, and they expire on their own.
  • Passwordless Windows Server logon. A custom Windows credential provider (C++) signs administrators in without a typed or stored password, for both domain and local accounts.
  • An AI agent gateway. AI agents can act only within limits a human has approved. I later rebuilt this idea from scratch as the public mcp-authz-gateway.
  • AI review of admin sessions. A self-hosted language model analyzes recorded privileged sessions, so recordings do not have to be sent to an outside AI service.

Passwordless RDP, in more detail

The login screen shows a QR code with a session ID, a cryptographic challenge, the server's identity and a timestamp. The user's device signs the challenge with its private key, and the auth server verifies the signature against the registered public key. No password is typed, sent or stored. It is a FIDO-inspired challenge-response flow, designed to work in air-gapped environments too.

Design principles

  • Remove the secret rather than guard it. A credential that does not exist cannot be stolen, replayed or left unrotated.
  • Access is granted just in time and expires by default. Nothing stays valid between uses.
  • The same model covers people, machines and AI agents. AI agents are the newest non-human identities and need the same limits.
